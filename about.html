About Himanshu Kumawat

First-year Civil Engineering Undergraduate | Fascinated by structural integrity, environmental sustainability, and modern computer-aided engineering.

My Philosophy & Story

Why Civil Engineering?

Ever since I was young, I looked up at towering bridges, multi-lane flyovers, and sky-touching high-rises and wondered: "How do humans translate simple lines on paper into monumental structures that withstand gravity, earthquakes, and time?"

Entering my first year of Bachelor of Technology in Civil Engineering, that curiosity quickly transformed into disciplined study. As a freshman, I am learning that civil engineering is the most fundamental discipline of civilization—providing water, shelter, transportation, and safety to communities.

My focus is currently two-fold: developing unshakeable competence in theoretical mechanics (equilibrium, truss resolution, stress distribution) and mastering modern digital tools like AutoCAD and MS Excel for structural estimation and precision detailing.

Areas of Exploration

My Freshman Engineering Focus

The four specialized domains of civil engineering I am actively immersing myself in during Year 1.

📐

AutoCAD & Graphics

Generating architectural floor plans, sectional elevations, column grids, and engineering drawings with disciplined layer hierarchies and standard line weights.

🏗️

Structural Mechanics

Analyzing concurrent and non-concurrent force systems, method of joints for pin-jointed trusses, bending moments, and static structural equilibrium.

🌱

Sustainable Concrete

Investigating cementitious replacements such as industrial fly ash, slag, and plastic aggregates to reduce embodied carbon in structural composites.

🧭

Surveying & Geomatics

Field surveying with Dumpy levels, chain and tape measurement, prismatic compass traversing, and generating topographic contour maps.

Beyond The Classroom

Activities & Mindset

Engineers need spatial awareness, precision, and patient problem solving. Outside lectures and laboratories, I enjoy activities that sharpen those same capabilities:

  • 🌉 Scale Model Prototyping: Constructing miniature truss bridges, arches, and load-bearing balsa structures.
  • ♟️ Chess: Enhancing tactical foresight, patience, and pattern recognition.
  • 📷 Architectural Observation: Photographing heritage masonry, arches, and urban stormwater systems.
  • 📖 Engineering Case Studies: Reading post-mortem analyses of famous engineering feats and structural failures.

"Measure Twice, Cut Once."

As a first-year student, I approach every lab practical, surveying station, and AutoCAD drawing with rigorous verification and respect for engineering standards.
